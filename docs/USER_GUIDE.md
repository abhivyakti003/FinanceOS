# User guide

## Getting started

1. **Create account:** name, email, password, accept the Terms, then enter the 6-digit code
   emailed to you. Check spam if it doesn't arrive within a minute.
2. **Add your money**, in any of three ways:
   - **Accounts → Link bank automatically** (sample data until live bank linking is enabled).
   - **Import statement:** download a statement from net banking (CSV, Excel or PDF) and upload it.
     Overlapping statements are fine; duplicates are skipped.
   - **Transactions → Add a transaction** for cash and anything else.

## Pages

| Page | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | This month at a glance, what needs attention, suggestions, 12-month trend. Pick a category to see its transactions. |
| Transactions | Filter, search, change categories right in the table (FinanceOS learns the merchant), delete. |
| Insights | Spending patterns for any period: categories, top merchants, largest and unusual payments. |
| Accounts | Add accounts, fix a balance to match your bank, rename, delete, link banks. |
| Import statement | Upload bank statements; preview before importing. |
| Investments | Add holdings or import your mutual fund CAS; edit values in the table. |
| Bills | Due dates, reminders, mark paid, snooze. Turn detected subscriptions into bills. |
| Recurring payments | Subscriptions, rent, SIPs and EMIs found automatically. |
| Budgets | Monthly limits per category with progress. |
| Goals | Targets, progress, time to reach them, monthly amount needed. |
| Net worth | Accounts + investments + other assets − loans. |
| Credit score | Record scores from the bureau's free check and see the trend. |
| Family | Share your finances read-only with family, and view what they share with you. |
| Reports | Download a PDF summary for any period. |
| Settings | Email preferences, test email, password, recent activity, download or delete your data. |

## Tips

- **Transfers between your own accounts** and credit card bill payments are detected so
  they don't count as spending. Fix any mistakes by setting the category to Transfer.
- **Investments count as savings**, not spending.
- **Light or dark mode:** ⋮ menu at the top right → Settings → Theme.

## Privacy

Only you see your data, unless you share it with family. You can download everything or
delete your account at any time from Settings.
