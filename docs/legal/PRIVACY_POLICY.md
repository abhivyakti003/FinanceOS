**{OPERATOR}** ("we") operates FinanceOS. This policy explains what personal data we
collect, why, and the rights you have.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, email address and a one-way hash of your password.
  We can't see your password.
- **Financial data you add:** bank and card accounts, transactions (from statements you
  upload, entries you type, or bank links you approve), budgets, goals, investments,
  bills, loans, assets and credit scores you record.
- **Security records:** login times, failed login attempts and security events such as
  password changes, kept to protect your account.

We do **not** collect your net banking passwords, card numbers, CVV or UPI PIN.

## Why we use it (purpose)

Only to provide FinanceOS to you: showing your balances and spending, categorising
transactions, reminders, reports, and keeping your account secure. We don't sell your
data, show ads, or share it with anyone for marketing.

## Consent

You give consent when you create an account. You can withdraw it at any time by
deleting your account in **Settings → Delete my account**, which erases your data.

## Who can see your data

- **You.**
- **Family members you invite**, read-only, until you stop sharing.
- **Administrators** see your name, email, sign-up date, last login and how many
  accounts and transactions you have, never amounts or details.
- **Service providers** that help us run FinanceOS (hosting, email delivery, and, if you
  link a bank, a licensed Account Aggregator). They may only use the data to provide
  their service to us.
- Authorities, when the law requires it.

## Bank linking (Account Aggregator)

If you link a bank, you approve each connection in your Account Aggregator app. Access
is read-only and you can revoke it at any time in FinanceOS or in the AA app.

## Storage and security

Data is stored in {DATA_LOCATION}. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt; verification codes
are hashed; connections use HTTPS; access is limited to what each person needs; and
data is backed up regularly.

## How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is erased
immediately from the live database and from backups within {BACKUP_DAYS} days. Security
log entries may be kept for up to 1 year.

## Your rights (DPDP Act)

You can: see and download your data (**Settings → Your data**), correct it, delete it,
withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you, and complain to us or to the Data
Protection Board of India.
